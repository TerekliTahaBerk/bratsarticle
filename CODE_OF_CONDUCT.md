# Code of conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, evaluate ideas on evidence, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private
information. Scientific disagreements should be documented with reproducible
tests or source citations.

Patient privacy and data-use terms are part of acceptable conduct. Do not
commit restricted MRI data, identifiers, credentials, or model artifacts whose
distribution is not authorized.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The maintainer may
edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when these
expectations are violated.
