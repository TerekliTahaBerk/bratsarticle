## Recommended order

1. **Medical Image Analysis** if the controlled results yield a general lesson
   about architecture attribution that transfers beyond this dataset.
2. **Radiology: Artificial Intelligence** if the strongest contribution is
   independent African/scanner-domain validation and failure analysis that is
   interpretable to medical-imaging readers.
3. **IEEE TMI** only if the work demonstrates a substantial methodological
   contribution beyond applying established components.
4. **Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics** or **Computer Methods and
   Programs in Biomedicine** as scope-aligned alternatives.

No journal is selected at this stage because the central external result does
not exist. A Q1/Q2 acceptance outcome is never guaranteed.

## Mandatory pre-submission recheck

- Current JCR/Scopus quartile and category.
- Exact article type, length, figure/table and supplement limits.
- Open-access fees and institutional agreements.
- Data/code/model-weight policy.
- Generative-AI disclosure policy.
- Prior-submission disclosure rules.
- Required CLAIM/checklist and author declarations.
