# Author confirmation required

The following information cannot be inferred safely from code or experiment
artifacts and must be confirmed by the authors before submission:

- complete author list, order, affiliations and ORCID identifiers;
- corresponding-author address and email;
- CRediT contributions for every author;
- funding bodies, grant numbers and funder roles;
- conflicts of interest;
- institutional ethics/IRB interpretation and waiver status for each reused
  public dataset;
- acknowledgements;
- journal-specific generative-AI disclosure;
- permission to distribute trained checkpoints;
- final target journal and current institutional APC agreement.

No placeholder statement in the submission package should be interpreted as an
author declaration.

The conditional target analysis is in
`submission/journal_target_rationale.md`. If Radiology: Artificial
Intelligence is selected, the current verified main-paper limits are frozen in
`submission/radiology_ai_original_research_contract.yaml` but must still be
rechecked immediately before submission.
